FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer free quotes?

Yes. All our quotes are free and with no obligation. For most jobs we visit the site to measure and discuss your requirements before sending a written quote.

Are you insured?

Yes, Art Brickwork Ltd is fully insured with public liability insurance for all the work we carry out.

Do you supply the bricks and materials?

We can supply all materials, including matching bricks for extensions and repairs, or work with materials you have already chosen.

Do you work for builders and developers?

Yes. We work with homeowners as well as main contractors, builders and developers on new-build and commercial projects.
