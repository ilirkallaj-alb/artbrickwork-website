About Art Brickwork Ltd

Who we are

Built on skill, pride and trust

Art Brickwork Ltd is a specialist bricklaying company providing high-quality brickwork and masonry for private homeowners, main contractors and developers.

Our team of time-served bricklayers has worked on everything from small garden walls and repairs to extensions, new-build homes and commercial projects. Whatever the size of the job, we bring the same attention to detail, clean working and honest communication.

We believe good brickwork should not only be strong, but also beautiful — with neat joints, accurate lines and materials chosen to suit your property.
